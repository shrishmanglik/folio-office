# Community conduct

Be respectful, specific and constructive. Discuss ideas and code without personal attacks. Welcome people of different backgrounds and levels of experience.

Harassment, threats, discrimination, unwanted sexual attention, doxxing and posting another person's private data are not acceptable. Do not use project spaces to spam or deceive.

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and restrict participation. Report a concern privately through an available GitHub contact channel to a maintainer; do not repost private information in an issue. Decisions should protect participants and explain the relevant behavior when safe to do so.
